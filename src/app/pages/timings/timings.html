Sacred Dress Code

Traditional attire is encouraged. Devotees are requested to dress modestly. Footwear must be deposited at the free footwear counter.

📱

Mobile & Photography

Photography inside the inner sanctum (Garbha Griha) is prohibited to preserve sanctity. Please keep phones on silent mode.

🌍

Global Devotees & NRI Care

Devotees outside India can attend morning & evening Aartis live online. All time slots automatically convert to your local timezone.
