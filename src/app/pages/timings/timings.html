Sacred Dress Code

Traditional Indian attire is encouraged. Devotees are requested to dress modestly. Footwear must be deposited at the free footwear counter.

📱

Mobile & Photography

Photography inside the inner sanctum (Garbha Griha) is prohibited to preserve sanctity. Please keep phones on silent mode.

🪔

Prasad Distribution

Satvik Charanamrit and blessed dry fruit prasad is distributed after every morning Rajbhog Aarti and evening Sandhya Aarti.
